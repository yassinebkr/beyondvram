Write a short introduction to the history of the printing press.

The printing press, a revolutionary invention that transformed the dissemination of information, has its roots in the early 15th century. While the concept of printing dates back to ancient civilizations, the modern printing press as we know it today was pioneered by Johannes Gutenberg in the mid-1400s. Gutenberg's invention, which combined movable type with a mechanical press, marked a pivotal moment in history, enabling the mass production of books and the rapid spread of knowledge. This breakthrough not only democratized information but also laid the groundwork for the cultural, scientific, and intellectual advancements that would shape the modern world.
